Protocol
Protein First. Always. No Exceptions.
When I cut from 240lbs to sub-10% body fat, I never let protein drop below 1g per pound of bodyweight. People slash calories but lose muscle and just end up smaller. Keep protein high, cut carbs and fat moderately, trust the process.

Protocol
Sleep Is The Steroid You're Ignoring
More growth hormone is released in the first 2 hours of deep sleep than at any other time. Missing sleep doesn't just make you tired — it destroys your testosterone, spikes cortisol, and kills your results. 7-9 hours isn't optional. It's the protocol.

Christopher · Founder
🍗 Nutrition
Carb Timing
Front-Load Your Carbs Around Training
Pre-workout carbs are your fuel. Post-workout carbs are your recovery. I always front-loaded around training and kept them lower at night. This one shift changed my body composition more than anything else I did. Carb timing isn't bro science — it works.
